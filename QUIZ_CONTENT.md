## Editing and review

Each file is named for its YouTube video ID. Keep question and option IDs stable; increment `revision` whenever a question, correct answer, or meaning changes. New revisions begin fresh attempts and leave old records intact. Cosmetic edits that do not change meaning can keep the revision.

Check that each question has one unambiguous answer supported by its episode, plausible distractors, and a short original explanation. Keep wording supportive. Avoid presenting the course’s historical scientific claims as current clinical guidance. The exercise questions cover the episode’s broad framing, not medical prescriptions.

`seconds` links to a relevant section when its timestamp is available. Zero deliberately links to the beginning of the original video; it does not claim an exact location. External review links open a new tab so the current quiz remains in place; watching there does not update local watch progress.

Run `pnpm fmt` and `pnpm build` after editing. The content test checks all ten pilot episodes and runtime validators reject mismatched video IDs, duplicate IDs, absent correct options, and invalid timestamps. Manually check the changed question and its source before publication.
