Watch Crash Course is an unofficial, unaffiliated fan-made website that makes it a little easier to find your way through great educational videos.

Great videos. A clearer path.

Explore science, history, philosophy, technology, and learning skills, with numbered episodes organized into complete courses. Pick a lesson and watch through YouTube’s official embedded player, with your place saved in this browser.

Pick up where you left off, mark lessons complete, and see your progress and history. Download or restore a backup to carry your progress to another browser.

Free to learn. Free to build on.

This site is 100% free to use. Its original code and artwork are open-source under the MIT license. Crash Course’s videos remain their own work.

No accounts. No analytics. Your saved place, completed episodes, and history stay in this browser; they don’t sync between devices.

Clearing site data or using private browsing can erase progress. Back up your data before moving to another browser.

If saving fails, we’ll show a warning. You can keep watching with temporary progress in the current tab. If you watch the same video in several tabs, the latest save sets your place.

YouTube connects only after you choose to load a video. Its privacy practices apply to the player and to links you follow there. Watching directly on YouTube won’t update your progress here.

Our hosting provider receives normal web requests when you visit.
